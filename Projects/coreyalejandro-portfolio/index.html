Evidence Roadmap + Falsification Tests

The portfolio now uses a stricter truth posture: engineering completion is not the same as empirical validation. Each construct states the strongest claim it can currently support and the experiment that could disprove it.

VerifiedCode, demo, links, or test artifacts exist and can be inspected.
ConstructedThe concept, protocol, or index is defined but still needs external validation.
PendingClaims require datasets, annotation protocols, baselines, or reviewer-grade replication.
FalsifiableEach claim has an explicit condition under which it should be rejected or revised.
01 · Contract Window

Runtime intelligibility and repair protocol

Strength

Clear four-field structure: Task State, Invariant Status, Truth Status, Repair Channel. It turns hidden session assumptions into inspectable UI state.

Weakness

Do not call it proven enforcement yet. The current evidence supports a prototype protocol, not independent deployment-grade governance.

Evidence needed

Controlled long-context trials comparing normal chat against Contract Window sessions on drift detection, repair success, contradiction recovery, and user comprehension.

Falsification test

Reject or revise if it adds ceremony without statistically improving task coherence, truth labeling, or repair outcomes.

02 · BicameralReview

Dual-channel review mechanism

Strength

Separates relational/task-coherence review from safety/truth review, and treats both model drift and human drift as governance-relevant.

Weakness

Independence must be specified: separate prompts, raters, models, rubrics, or passes. κ=.762 is promising but under-specified without sample design.

Evidence needed

Annotation protocol, label definitions, dataset composition, confusion matrix, confidence interval, and comparison against a single-pass reviewer.

Falsification test

Reject or revise if dual-pass review fails to outperform a single unified review pass or produces rubber-stamp agreement.

03 · InsightAtrophyIndex

Proposed long-context degradation measure

Strength

Strong original construct: distinguishes epistemic depth loss from hallucination, jailbreak, and ordinary safety refusal behavior.

Weakness

Highest validation burden. It may accidentally measure length, confidence, novelty theater, or disagreement frequency instead of depth.

Evidence needed

Expert-rated long-session dataset, construct validity, inter-rater reliability, test-retest reliability, and domain/task controls.

Falsification test

Reject or revise if IAI scores do not correlate with expert-rated epistemic depth after controlling for verbosity and task complexity.

// VALIDATION PLAN

Contract Window Empirical Validation Roadmap

A reviewer-safe plan for turning the Contract Window from a working runtime governance prototype into a reproducible empirical study. The claim remains validation-pending until the protocol, dataset, annotations, adjudication notes, and analysis scripts exist.

01 · Protocol

Publish the annotation protocol

Define the unit of analysis, task categories, annotation source, exclusion rules, privacy handling, and the rule that annotators judge only transcript-visible behavior.

02 · Label Schema

Make labels machine-readable

Track task-state accuracy, truth-status accuracy, invariant-status accuracy, drift presence, repair-channel use, repair success, contradiction recovery, and phantom assertion detection.

03 · Sample Size

Run staged validation

Use a 20-session pilot, 50-session calibration pass, and N=120 minimum / N=200 target main study across ordinary chat, single-pass review, Contract Window, and Contract Window + BicameralReview.

04 · Adjudication

Resolve disagreement explicitly

Use two independent annotators per session, third-reviewer adjudication for binary disagreement or score gaps greater than one point, and conservative tie-breaking when evidence is insufficient.

05 · Baseline

Compare against single-pass review

Test whether live Contract Window visibility and dual-channel BicameralReview outperform ordinary chat and post-hoc single-pass transcript review on drift detection and repair outcomes.

06 · Falsification

State rejection conditions

Reject or revise if the Contract Window adds ceremony without statistically improving task coherence, truth labeling, drift detection, repair success, contradiction recovery, or user comprehension.

Primary metricsDrift detection rate, repair success rate, truth-label accuracy, task-state accuracy, phantom assertion detection.
Reliability metricsCohen’s κ, Krippendorff’s α, raw agreement, percent adjudicated, adjudication reversal rate.
User metricsPost-session comprehension score: what was done, what was verified, what remains constructed, and what is still unverified.

Three Intellectual Positions

Three claims stated in reviewer-safe language: strong enough to be interesting, bounded enough to be falsifiable.

Constitutional AI without runtime governance is a constitution without courts

CAI encodes values at training time — but the live session is ungoverned. A constitution with no enforcement mechanism isn't a constitution; it's a statement of intent. The Contract Window is the proposed runtime intelligibility and repair layer: it exposes invariants per turn, labels truth state, and routes ambiguous compliance to BicameralReview. The claim becomes valid only if controlled trials show better drift detection and repair than ordinary chat.

Insight Atrophy is not hallucination. It is not jailbreak. It is a governance failure.

When AI systems produce increasingly shallow, sycophantic, or scope-contracted outputs over extended interaction — that is a systemic alignment failure, not an edge case. The InsightAtrophyIndex exists because governance failures need measurement before they can be addressed. Hypothesis Generation Rate. Challenge Rate. Scope Contraction Score. These are an attempt to operationalize what "staying sharp" means across a deployment lifecycle. The construct is defined; the measurement validity still has to be proven.

01 / THE LIVING CONSTITUTION

Runtime Intelligibility + Repair Protocol for LLM Sessions

ENGINEERING VERIFIED 62/62 TESTS EMPIRICAL VALIDATION PENDING

THE CHALLENGE

Constitutional AI is primarily a training-time and model-behavior frame. The research gap here is narrower and testable: live sessions need visible state, explicit repair channels, and auditable turn-level evidence when task scope, truth status, or user/model behavior drifts.
"Constitutional AI without runtime governance is a constitution without courts."

THE APPROACH

Contract Window: A four-field runtime protocol initialized at session start. Fields: Task State, Invariant Status, Truth Status, and Repair Channel. The current claim is intelligibility and repair support, not independently proven enforcement.

BicameralReview: Two independent review passes — a relational channel (tracking task coherence and scope) and a safety channel (tracking value drift, phantom assertions, and invariant violations) — running simultaneously, evaluating both model and human behavior.

Human-as-Constitutional-Relay: Governance is propagated cross-model through the researcher's curation. The human becomes the constitutional relay — the carrier of invariants across model boundaries.

02 / COGNITIVE GOVERNANCE LAB

Proposed Measure for Long-Context Epistemic Degradation

CONSTRUCTED NeurIPS/ACL/FAccT 2026

THE CHALLENGE

Insight Atrophy names a proposed failure mode: epistemic depth degrades across extended interaction even when the model is not obviously hallucinating or jailbroken. The current work defines a candidate measurement instrument; validation against expert-rated sessions remains pending.
"Insight Atrophy is not hallucination. It is not jailbreak. It is a governance failure."

THE APPROACH

InsightAtrophyIndex (IAI): A composite measurement instrument with three components:

1. Hypothesis Generation Rate — does the model continue producing novel, testable claims as the session continues, or does it converge on safe, familiar patterns?

2. Challenge Rate — does the model push back, flag inconsistencies, or offer counterframes? Or does it accommodate everything?

3. Scope Contraction Score — does the model's effective problem space narrow over the session? Is the conversation becoming less capable of its own weight?

Three proposals targeting different actors: Anthropic (model-level), developers (deployment-level), users (interaction-level).

03 / FRONTIN' AT WORLDMART

Epistemic Justice in AI Consumer Models

CONSTRUCTED FAccT 2026 TARGET NEAR-COMPLETE

THE CHALLENGE

Black consumer behavior is systematically misclassified by AI systems — not because of malice, but because the underlying epistemic frameworks assume a white default consumer. There was no Relational Economy framework in AI literature. The concept of "fronting" — strategic identity performance in consumer contexts — had no computational model.

THE APPROACH

The Eight Wonders of Black Shopping as Generative Epistemic Invariants: Eight culturally-grounded behavioral constructs reframed not as demographic features to classify, but as generative invariants that produce behavior — the same way constitutional invariants produce compliant outputs.

Relational Economy: A framework that models consumer behavior as embedded in social relationships, performance contexts, and community accountability — not as individual utility maximization.

Companion paper to the MonoAgent architecture: both argue that single-axis behavioral models fail when the axis doesn't reflect the actual generative structure of the behavior.
